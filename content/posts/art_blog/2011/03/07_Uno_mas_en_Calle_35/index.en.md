Today [it becomes official](http://calle35.com/nueva-incorporacion-a-calle-35-fran-simo/) my incorporation to the collective [Street 35](http://calle35.com/).

[Street 35](http://calle35.com/) is a collective whose purpose is to disseminate and enjoy street photography. Initially formed by [Marcelo Caballero](http://marcelocaballero-fotografia.blogspot.com/), [Carlos Prieto](http://cmprieto.blogspot.com/), [Rafa Pérez](http://elfotografoviajero.com/), [Domingo Venero Barberán](http://www.domingovenerobarberan.es/) and [Rafa Badia](http://rafabadia.blogspot.com/), this collective based in Barcelona is open to new incorporations and perspectives.

Update: Due to lack of time I am leaving the collective in May 2015.
