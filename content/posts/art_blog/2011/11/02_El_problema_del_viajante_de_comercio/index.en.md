Reviewing the drawer of memories I found the final project I did for my degree of «University Analyst of
Information Systems», an intermediate degree in the engineering program at the National Technological University.

It is a [heuristic](http://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heur%C3%ADstica) approach using
[geo‑referenced](http://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georreferenciaci%C3%B3n) information for the classic
«[Traveling Salesman Problem](http://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Problema_del_viajante)». The intention is for it to be easy to
implement in a business environment with standard SQL servers.

It has been available online for a long time and I have wanted to rescue it.
