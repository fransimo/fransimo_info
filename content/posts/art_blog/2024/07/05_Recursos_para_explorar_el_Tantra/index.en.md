People often ask me about Tantra: what is it, where to start? Tantra is an experience that must be lived in the body. For those just starting, I like to recommend a "Tantra for Everyone". Each facilitator has their own approach, but they are usually shorter, introductory meetings.

Here is a list of facilitators so you can explore what Tantra is for them:

I fully trust these professionals and have learned a lot from them.

Many colleagues from Verma’s training offer a more personalized "Tantric path", with private, individual or couple sessions. You can find nearby therapists on the [school’s website](https://escueladaya.com/terapeutas/).

### Meditations

Meditation is central to Tantra. Osho popularized active meditations, which, while not the only ones in Tantra, are closely related and can be practiced solo:

- [Osho.com - Active Meditations](https://www.osho.com/es/meditation/osho-active-meditations/introduction)
- [YouTube - Active Meditations](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDtuqx9znDF_fcYu5xUuNktymiIaC7THF)
- [Spotify - Heart Chakra Meditation Music](https://open.spotify.com/album/3ispYAtud2YDxS9iWwwtS3?si=AsL1vw0WQ7KmsKI8qVigpA)
- [Tandava on YouTube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDtuqx9znDF_7Noe_wIIE1ZWnfzK62zaY)
