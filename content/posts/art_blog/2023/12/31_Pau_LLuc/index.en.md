Pau Lluc was a pseudonym I used in 2024 to _explore detachment, maintain online privacy, and avoid conditioning the massage experience with my digital identity_.

I did not want a massage client to associate me with my work in artificial intelligence. I still do not understand why. What I do know is that at this moment my goal is to integrate art, healing, and computer science, and one of the steps is to integrate digital identities.
<--->
## About Pau Lluc

The name combines two [toponyms](https://dle.rae.es/top%C3%B3nimo). The name of the city where I was born contains the word peace. Many of my walks have as destination or passing place [Lluc](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monasterio_de_Santa_Mar%C3%ADa_(Lluch)), where I have lived many important moments.

**The name intends to bring the essence of _"peace of the sacred forest"_.** A place to find oneself and to see the world from a different perspective, to return to it and act from a center and certainty based on love. Neither the way out nor the way back has to be peaceful.
