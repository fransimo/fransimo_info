The photographs of [Inside Out](http://i-o-p.es/) expose two inner worlds. The paths chosen by the authors diverge when selecting the forms and elements of the photographic language. Outside, the search is made in the large structures. Inside, in the small everyday things. They come together in similar compositional lines that create contrasting pairs.

**Date:** From April 19 to May 18, 2007

**Location:** [Escola Da Vinci](http://davinci-barcelona.com/), C/ Cucurulla 9 – 1º, 08002 Barcelona [(map)](http://maps.google.es/maps?f=q&hl=es&q=c/cucurulla+9,08002,+barcelona&sll=39.46684,-0.377954&sspn=0.011248,0.019913&layer=&ie=UTF8&om=1&z=16&iwloc=addr)

**Price:** Free admission
