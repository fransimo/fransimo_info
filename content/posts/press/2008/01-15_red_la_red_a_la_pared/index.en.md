Barcelona Photobloggers leaves the online world again to present its second group exhibition titled "From the Network to the Wall II" on January 17 at 8:30 p.m. A total of 34 photobloggers from the Barcelona area will display their photographs at the Fotonauta gallery until February 8.

The initiative followed the participatory philosophy of the internet, allowing each author to select the photographs to exhibit. All the work of organization, coordination, and printing of photographs was also carried out through the network.

The photographs will be auctioned during the days the exhibition runs, each starting at €20.
