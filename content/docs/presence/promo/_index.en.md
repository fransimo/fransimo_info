# Biodynamic Craniosacral

I’m in my third year of the [Biodynamic Craniosacral](../biodinamica_craneosacral) training and offer free sessions as part of my education.

Sessions are conducted in clothing, on a massage table. The contact is very gentle, non‑invasive, and involves minimal movement. The purpose is **to help the body connect with its natural capacity for self‑healing**.

Sessions last about an hour and consist of four parts: a brief initial conversation and three phases of contact where the practitioner’s hands remain in a fixed position.

The practitioner does not manipulate or channel energy into the client. The goal is to maintain a safe, judgment‑free space where the forces that organize the client’s system can express themselves, and through that expression find a new way to reorganize. This enhances self‑healing.

Pain and trauma often leave a mark on the body’s energetic flows. That mark can be alleviated, even if the client does not consciously remember the event that caused it.

**This promotion includes three free sessions**, which is the most common treatment. Sessions are usually scheduled at a frequency of two or three weeks.

# Which is better for me: Biodynamic or Tameana?

In this table you can see some key aspects of both therapies.

| Aspect | Biodynamic Craniosacral | Tameana |
|--------|------------------------|---------|
| Origin | Based on scientific studies and academic lineage | Channelled by Juan Manuel Giordano |
| Cultural roots | Osteopathic medicine, Europe and the U.S. | Cheroquí spiritual wisdom |
| Nature of the technique | Body therapy based on listening to the body | Vibrational energetic therapy with quartz crystals |
| Physical contact | Yes, very subtle, no movement | No contact required; can be done remotely |
| Practitioner’s role | Accompanies without directing the body’s process | Holds the space for energetic flow |
| Client participation | Can express bodily sensations if desired | No need to speak or share anything personal |
| Therapeutic focus | Facilitates the body’s self‑regulation | Aligns the recipient with their soul purpose |
| Training duration | 3 years, with continuous supervision | Brief and direct training |
| Theoretical base and bibliography | Extensive, with studies and publications | Non‑academic, based on direct experience and channeling |
| Expected impact | Internal reorganization, release of tensions and traumas | Energetic transformation at all levels |

I use these techniques because both have had a profound impact on my life when I received them as a client. They are non‑invasive and very subtle. They do not require the client to explain or know the origin of the _problem_. The therapist’s role is to facilitate a space, not to direct the process, judge, or recommend behaviors.

As a guide, we could say that if your interest is in the body and we can meet in person, the best option is biodynamic. If your motivation is on a more spiritual plane or we cannot meet, I would recommend Tameana.
